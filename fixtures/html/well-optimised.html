Handmade stoneware, thrown in Devon

Every piece on this site was thrown by hand in our studio near Ashcombe, then glazed and fired to 1280 degrees. That temperature matters: it vitrifies the clay body completely, which is what makes these pieces dishwasher safe and genuinely hard-wearing rather than decorative.

What we make

Dinner plates, side plates, cereal bowls, mugs and jugs, in three glazes: oatmeal, slate and a copper green that varies slightly between firings. Variation is inherent to the process and we do not consider it a fault.

Delivery and returns

Free UK delivery on orders over sixty pounds. Everything is packed in moulded pulp, not polystyrene. If a piece arrives damaged, send us a photograph and we will replace it.

Lead times

Items in stock ship within two working days. Commissioned sets take four to six weeks depending on the firing schedule.
